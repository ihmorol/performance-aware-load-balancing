# Devi et al. 2024, systematic review of load balancing and task scheduling

## Citation
Nisha Devi, Sandeep Dalal, Kamna Solanki, Surjeet Dalal, Umesh Kumar Lilhore, Sarita Simaiya, Nasratullah Nuristani. "A systematic literature review for load balancing and task scheduling techniques in cloud computing." Artificial Intelligence Review, vol. 57, article 276, 2024. DOI: 10.1007/s10462-024-10925-w (open access)

## Source
https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10462-024-10925-w.pdf. Local file: papers/2024_Devi_SLRLoadBalancingScheduling.pdf

## Problem
Review of optimization and machine learning methods for load balancing and task scheduling.

## Method
Systematic review of 63 English articles from 2014 to 2024, chosen with inclusion and exclusion criteria (abstract).

## Algorithms covered
Mainly metaheuristic and ML-based methods. Classic rules appear only as background.

## Key findings
51% of the reviewed studies used CloudSim, followed by Python at 11% (text with Fig. 10).

## Limitations
Observed: the scope leans to scheduling and ML, not proxy rules. Solid venue (Springer journal).

## Relevance
Evidence that the field relies on simulation.

## Gap our project could touch
A measured testbed comparison of plain rules.
