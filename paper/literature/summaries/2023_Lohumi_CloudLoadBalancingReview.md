# Lohumi et al. 2023, cloud load balancing review

## Citation
Yogesh Lohumi, Durgaprasad Gangodkar, Prakash Srivastava, Mohammad Zubair Khan, Abdulrahman Alahmadi, Ahmed H. Alahmadi. "Load Balancing in Cloud Environment: A State-of-the-Art Review." IEEE Access, vol. 11, pp. 134517-134530, 2023. DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3337146 (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)

## Source
https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10329329. Local file: papers/2023_Lohumi_CloudLoadBalancingReview.pdf

## Problem
Survey of load imbalance in cloud systems and the algorithms that address it.

## Method
Literature review with a taxonomy by system state, technique, function, and type (abstract).

## Algorithms covered
Static, dynamic, hybrid, and nature-inspired families.

## Metrics
Reviews parameters such as scalability, makespan, and throughput.

## Key findings
Reports that the reviewed work relies mostly on CloudSim and Cloud Analyst (Fig. 11) and that research is "primarily" simulation based.

## Limitations
Observed: the focus is VM and task scheduling, not request dispatching at a proxy. I found no tail latency discussion in the parts I read (abstract, taxonomy, simulation-tool section).

## Relevance
Citable static versus dynamic taxonomy for background.

## Gap our project could touch
Real VMs instead of simulation.
