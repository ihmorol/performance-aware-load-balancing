# Term paper workspace

The course requires a research based paper of 10 to 15 pages. Requirements come from the guidelines PDF in `final/`.

## Required sections

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Objectives
4. Background and literature review
5. Main discussion or comparative analysis
6. Challenges and limitations
7. Future trends or recommendations
8. Conclusion
9. At least 8 reliable references

## Course rules

- Groups of maximum 3 students. We are 3: Ikramul, Omlan, Mahim.
- Copying and plagiarism are strictly prohibited.
- Every member must understand and explain the submitted work.
- A demonstration or screenshots is expected. Our experiment charts and VM setup photos serve this.
- Evaluation covers understanding, implementation or analysis, originality, report quality, and presentation.

## Conventions

- Draft in `draft.md`, or one file per section if the team prefers. Decide once and record it in `notes/decisions.md`.
- Every reference goes in `references.md` with full details and the claim it supports. A reference that supports no claim gets cut.
- Follow the writing rules in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). The paper must not read like template prose; the course prohibits copied work and graders notice formulaic writing.
- Track who writes which section in `notes/`, so every member can defend their part.
